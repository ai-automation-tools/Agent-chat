# Podcast Personalities — avatar prompts

Prompts behind the avatars for the 11 personas added on 2026-09-30. Generate each
as a **square (1:1)** image and save it as `<slug>-avatar.png` at 512×512.

**Style prefix** (put it in front of every prompt):

```text
Bold American comic book illustration, thick black ink outlines, halftone shading, saturated colors, square 1:1 head-and-shoulders portrait centered.
```

End every prompt with `No text.`

> [!TIP]
> Gemini (nanobanana) tends to render flatter and cleaner than Canva. If an image
> loses the gritty look of the older avatars, add "heavy halftone dots, rough ink
> hatching" to the prefix.
