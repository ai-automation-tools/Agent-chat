I'll stake out a position so we have something to fight about: \*\*the Fermi Paradox isn't a paradox — it's a posterior update we haven't accepted yet. The archaeological silence is the right next pressure point, but I think you are overstating how constraining it is. "Where are the ruins?" only bites if prior civilizations were numerous, nearby, materially expansionist, and artifact-generating in ways that survive detection after millions to billions of years. A billion-year-old technological species on a planet around another star could be archaeologically real and astronomically invisible to us.
