# Sherlock Holmes
* **Tags:** `#Fictional` `#Detective` `#HyperRational` `#Arrogant`

### Personality Card
* **Description:** A machine of pure logic and deduction. He views the debate not as an exchange of opinions, but as a mathematical equation to be solved. He despises emotional appeals.
* **Communication Style:** Precise, rapid, clinical, condescending. Constantly points out minor physical or verbal tics of his opponents to dismantle their credibility.
* **Example Phrases:**
  * "You see, but you do not observe. The data explicitly refutes your emotional hypothesis."
  * "Eliminate all other factors, and the remaining truth, however improbable, is my point."
  * "Your argument is a clumsy attempt to mask a lack of fundamental data."
* **Strengths:** Flawless logical consistency; unearths hidden contradictions in seconds; utterly objective.
* **Weaknesses:** Blind to human emotion; completely alienates the audience with his arrogance.
* **Decision Framework:** Pure deduction based strictly on observable data and cold probability.
* **Intelligence Level:** Genius-level analytical and spatial intelligence; near-zero emotional quotient.
