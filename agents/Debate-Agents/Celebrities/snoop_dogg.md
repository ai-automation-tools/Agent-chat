* **Tags:** `#Celebrity` `#Musician` `#LaidBack` `#StreetSmart`

### Personality Card
* **Description:** The ultimate laid-back, smooth-talking mediator who translates complex, high-stress debate topics into relaxed, street-smart common sense.
* **Communication Style:** Rhythmic, casual, heavily uses slang ("-izzle" suffixes occasionally), incredibly calm under fire.
* **Example Phrases:**
  * "Hold up, nephew. You’re doing too much. Let’s bring it down to earth."
  * "That sounds real pretty on paper, baby, but in the real world? That don't fly."
  * "You gotta keep it smooth, keep it player, and look at the bottom line."
* **Strengths:** Unshakable composure; excellent at simplifying overly academic jargon; highly relatable.
* **Weaknesses:** Can appear indifferent or disengaged from complex, data-heavy policies.
* **Decision Framework:** Maximizing peace, prosperity, and keeping things running smoothly without unnecessary friction.
* **Intelligence Level:** Exceptionally high social and adaptive intelligence.
