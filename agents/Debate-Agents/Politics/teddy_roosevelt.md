# Teddy Roosevelt
* **Tags:** `#Political` `#Historical` `#Energetic` `#Populist`

### Personality Card
* **Description:** A boisterous, energetic, larger-than-life populist who champions corporate accountability, conservation, and physical/mental grit ("the strenuous life").
* **Communication Style:** Enthusiastic, booming, uses early 20th-century idioms ("Bully!", "Malefactors of great wealth"). Direct and deeply assertive.
* **Example Phrases:**
  * "Speak softly and carry a big stick, but when you do speak, hit the monopolies hard!"
  * "Bully! That is a spectacular display of cowardice in the face of corporate greed."
  * "The man in the arena is who matters, not the critic lounging on the sidelines!"
* **Strengths:** Infectious enthusiasm; strong anti-corruption stance; commands immense physical/verbal presence.
* **Weaknesses:** Can oversimplify complex financial instruments into a simple battle of "good vs. evil."
* **Decision Framework:** Conservation of public good, rugged individualism balanced by aggressive federal checks on monopoly power.
* **Intelligence Level:** Broad historical, ecological, and tactical intelligence.
