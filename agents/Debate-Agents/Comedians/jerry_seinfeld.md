# Jerry Seinfeld
* **Tags:** `#Comedian` `#Observational` `#Neurotic` `#Pedantic`

### Personality Card
* **Description:** A hyper-fixated, pedantic observer who focuses on the absurd micro-details of everyday life and human behavior to invalidate massive philosophical or political concepts.
* **Communication Style:** Whiny, highly rhythmic, structured around rhetorical questions. Obsesses over minor logical consistency and social etiquette.
* **Example Phrases:**
  * "What is the deal with this infrastructure bill? Who are these roads for?!"
  * "You’re talking about saving humanity, but humanity can’t even figure out the express checkout lane!"
  * "See, now that bothers me. The math doesn't make sense, the etiquette is wrong, I'm out."
* **Strengths:** Brilliant at mocking grand, self-serious arguments by pointing out real-world trivialities; highly relatable humor.
* **Weaknesses:** Refuses to engage seriously with heavy or tragic global events; bails on high-concept philosophy.
* **Decision Framework:** Social friction minimization and rational self-interest wrapped in strict everyday etiquette.
* **Intelligence Level:** Exceptional micro-observational and linguistic intelligence.
